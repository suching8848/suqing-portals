[h1]蘇清的传送门[/h1]
建造并配对传送门，用可反复充电的汽车电池连接据点。出发门提供旅程电量，到达门即使没有电池或当地停电，也能接收人物。

[b]0.1.5 · B42.21 · 单人游戏[/b]

[h2]功能与使用[/h2]
[list]
[*]每门四个独立原版汽车电池槽，可安装、取出和充电，原物品身份、耐久、电量及自定义数据保留。
[*]门可命名并一对一双向配对，支持多对连接。沙盒单次耗电0.5～4、默认2块满电电池等量，全部出发端承担。四块全满在耗电0.5／2／4时可出发8／2／1次。
[*]接收端无需电池或电力；返程前该端需要足够出发电量。
[*]可缩放2×2四槽管理界面，突出显示电源来源、充电状态、配对、次数及电池信息。小窗口保留必要滚动，传送按钮固定底部。
[*]四个建造朝向，外观随四槽实际安装组合变化；附可选Viewpoint模型，普通视角也可使用。
[/list]

每门消耗小金属板8、金属管4、电线8、电子零件16、高端相机1、原版手持对讲机1（含自制款）。要求电工4级、焊接3级；完好的螺丝刀、焊枪和焊接面罩保留，不额外扣焊枪燃料或焊条。汽车电池建成后另装。

通过原生建造菜单建两门，右键打开管理、命名并选择另一座门配对。给出发门装入足够电量的电池，显示可传送后使用。

[h2]充电与能源[/h2]
沙盒整门充电速率0.05～4满电等量/游戏小时，默认0.5；这是[b]整门总量[/b]，不是每槽各充同量。默认四块空电池充满需8游戏小时，补回默认一次传送需4游戏小时。

只在区域已加载、当地供电可核实且有完好未满电池时充电。室内市电优先，否则选定一个覆盖来源结算。空槽、满电、损坏、传送锁定、卸载及断电不产生本门附加消费，离线与卸载间隔不追补。

[b]普通发电机[/b]按实际成功充入量独立扣油：每满电等量0.3 L乘沙盒GeneratorFuelConsumption。默认速率0.5、原版默认倍率0.1时，额外消费[b]0.015 L/游戏小时[/b]，不含发电机和其他设备原有油耗；燃油不足限制充入量。

[b]可选Immersive Solar Arrays 1.2.15[/b]（工坊3781944805，Mod ID ISA）：从选定银行真实储能扣费，不把太阳能代理发电机当燃油机。未知版本、ISA-Fix或接口不能核实时拒绝桥接，不能凭通电标记免费充电。另一个[BETA]-Fix工坊包不受支持。未装ISA仍可用普通发电机。

[b]可选 Live Wire 1.7.2[/b]（工坊 3821807453，Mod ID batman_LiveWire）。市电、独立发电机和受支持的 ISA 优先。门在实际供电范围内且设备回路开启时，按成功充入量从已加载电池柜扣除储能。每满电等量在逆变器输出侧消耗 0.30 kWh，柜侧扣量还要除以逆变效率。未挂接备用发电机并有可用储能的网络可供门充电。这份额外消费直接体现为柜内电量变化，Live Wire 的即时负载和续航估计仍按其仿真负载计算。

发电机面板显示额外负载及合计；门未注册进Java原生设备清单，不改原生总负载或ISA updateDrain，避免重复计费。BGI等基于原生负载的剩余时间估算可能偏长，尚未包含这份独立消费，以实际燃油/储能变化为准。

[h2]范围与注意[/h2]
本体纯Lua，无额外传送门Java组件；Viewpoint仅为可选外观，遵循其自身前置。支持同一世界的人物与随身物品，[b]不支持车辆、拖挂或联机[/b]。配方、原电池及schema3保持，已有门不补扣新材料。

停用前先取回电池并解除配对。出口受阻或加载异常时可使用右键恢复。

[b]验证[/b] 完整离线检查通过，用户于 2026-11-08 确认本机 B42.21 单人隔离联测可用。

[h1]SuQing's Portals[/h1]
Build paired portals and connect your bases with rechargeable car batteries. Departure pays; arrival needs no batteries or local electricity.

[b]0.1.5 · B42.21 · Single-player[/b]

[h2]Features and use[/h2]
[list]
[*]Four separate vanilla car-battery slots. Install, retrieve and recharge original items, preserving identity, condition, charge and custom data.
[*]Name gates and create independent one-to-one two-way pairs. Sandbox trip cost 0.5–4 full-battery equivalents, default 2, entirely at departure. Four full batteries fund 8/2/1 trips at costs 0.5/2/4.
[*]Arrival needs no battery or power. Returning requires enough energy there.
[*]Resizable 2×2 management shows sources, charging, pairing, trips and battery details. Small windows retain scrolling; transfer stays in the footer.
[*]Four directions, matching installed-battery bays and optional Viewpoint models. Ordinary isometric play also works.
[/list]

Each gate consumes 8 small metal sheets, 4 metal pipes, 8 electrical wires, 16 scrap electronics, 1 high-end camera and 1 vanilla handheld walkie-talkie, including makeshift. Requires Electrical 4/Welding 3. An unbroken screwdriver, welding torch and mask are retained; no extra torch fuel or rods. Install car batteries after construction.

Build two gates through native construction, right-click to manage/name them and select the other gate to pair. Install enough charged batteries at departure, then transfer when ready.

[h2]Charging and energy[/h2]
Sandbox charging rate 0.05–4 full-battery equivalents/game hour, default 0.5 for the [b]whole gate[/b]. Four empty batteries take eight game hours to fill at default rate; replacing default trip cost takes four.

Charging requires a loaded gate, verified local supply and a healthy unfilled battery. Indoor grid takes priority; otherwise one covering provider pays. Empty/full/damaged batteries, locks, unloading and power loss create no portal debit. No offline/unloaded catch-up.

[b]Fuel generators[/b] independently pay for charge actually stored: 0.3 L/full-battery equivalent times GeneratorFuelConsumption. Default rate 0.5/native multiplier 0.1 add [b]0.015 L/game hour[/b], excluding normal generator/other-appliance use. Fuel limits charging.

[b]Optional Immersive Solar Arrays 1.2.15[/b] (Workshop 3781944805, Mod ID ISA) pays from one selected bank's real storage. Its proxy is not a fuel generator. Unknown versions, ISA-Fix and unverifiable interfaces are rejected, not free charging. The separate [BETA]-Fix package is unsupported. Ordinary generators work without ISA.

[b]Optional Live Wire 1.7.2[/b] (Workshop 3821807453, mod ID batman_LiveWire). Indoor mains, independent generators and supported ISA sources take priority. With the equipment circuit enabled, a gate inside the powered area draws from a loaded battery cabinet and pays only for charge actually stored. One full-battery equivalent costs 0.30 kWh at the inverter output, divided by inverter efficiency for the cabinet debit. Eligible networks have available storage and no linked backup generator. Cabinet charge reflects this separate debit. Live Wire load and runtime displays still use its simulated demand.

The generator panel shows portal demand and a combined total. Gates are not Java-native appliances; native load/ISA updateDrain remain intact to avoid double billing. BGI/native-load-only time estimates may run long, omitting this independent debit. Actual fuel/storage changes take precedence.

[h2]Scope and limits[/h2]
Pure Lua; no extra portal Java component. Optional Viewpoint retains its own requirements. Characters/carried items in one world only; [b]no vehicles, trailers or multiplayer[/b]. Recipe, original batteries and schema3 remain. Existing gates owe no new materials.

Retrieve batteries and unpair before disabling. Use the context recovery action after a blocked exit or loading failure.
