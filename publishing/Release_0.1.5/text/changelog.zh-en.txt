0.1.5 · B42.21 单人 / Single-player

新增 Live Wire 1.7.2 可选供电。门在有电且设备回路开启的覆盖内按实际充入量扣除电池柜储能，管理界面显示「Live Wire 储能」。
支持带可用电池柜、未挂接备用发电机的网络。市电、独立发电机和已验证的 ISA 来源优先。

Added optional Live Wire 1.7.2 charging from a powered network with the equipment circuit enabled. The management window identifies Live Wire storage, and cabinet energy is debited only for charge actually stored.
Eligible networks have available cabinet storage and no linked backup generator. Mains, independent generators and supported ISA sources take priority.
